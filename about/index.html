About

I am a Professor of Philosophy at San Francisco State University. Before coming to SF State I was an Associate Professor in the Physics Department at the Federal University of Juiz de Fora, in Minas Gerais, Brazil. I am a Corresponding Member of the Brazilian Academy of Philosophy.

I was trained as a physicist — my master's degree and doctorate are both from the Brazilian Center for Research in Physics (CBPF) in Rio de Janeiro — and my work has moved steadily from physics toward the philosophical questions that physics raises without settling.
