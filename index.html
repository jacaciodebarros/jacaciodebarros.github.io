J. Acacio de Barros

Professor of Philosophy, San Francisco State University

Foundations of Physics. Philosophy of Science. Probability. Cognition and Mind.

I work on the foundations and philosophy of physics, particularly quantum mechanics. Recently I have been working with Federico Holik and Décio Krause on the indistinguishability of quantum particles, and on what a lack of identity for particles means for contextuality and for the negative probabilities I use to describe it.

I am also interested in the mind and the brain: in modeling brain function and cognition from physically plausible assumptions, and in how quantum physics meets consciousness, if at all. Both lines of work lead to the same questions about probability, contextuality, and what counts as an explanation.
